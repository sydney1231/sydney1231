# Kgolagano Sydney Lefifi

**Architect (PrArch, SACAP)** · Director, LEMEG Architects · Pretoria, South Africa

I design healthcare and public infrastructure in South Africa. My approach is research-led and data-informed: design decisions are tested against evidence, standards and measurable performance before they are drawn.

## Focus areas

- **Healthcare architecture** – end-to-end design and implementation of healthcare facilities
- **Healthcare research** – evidence-based design, clinical planning, space standards and post-occupancy learning
- **Infrastructure** – public-sector buildings and the systems that support them
- **Design technology** – BIM (Revit), computational design and data workflows
- **Forward-looking design** – using data to inform resilient, adaptable buildings
